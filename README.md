## 👋 About Me

<img align="right" alt="Coding" width="340" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/229223263-cf2e4b07-2615-4f87-9c38-e37600f8381a.gif">

I'm an **AI Engineer** working at the intersection of **robotics** and **machine learning**, with a background in Robotics Engineering and a focus on autonomous systems — perception, planning, and control on real hardware.

Most of my work is about getting deep learning models out of the notebook and onto devices that actually run: self-driving platforms, mobile robots, and embedded vision systems. I care about the problems where an algorithm has to hold up under real constraints — latency, compute budget, and reliability.

- 🚗 **Now building** — perception for autonomous vehicles, deep learning on edge devices
- 🔬 **Interested in** — agentic AI, generative models, world models, graph neural networks, sensor fusion, planning under uncertainty
- 🤝 **Open to** — collaboration on serious robotics / AI projects
